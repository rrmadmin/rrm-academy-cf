Paired Type Scale — lock the optical relationship once

Cormorant Garamond has a low x-height and high contrast; Inter has a tall x-height. At equal px they read as different sizes, which is why per-page compensation crept in (75 distinct font-sizes live today). This locks 11 steps + 8 line-heights as the complete vocabulary; the remaining 222 type-scale findings then snap mechanically to the nearest step.
