Each day she notes two things: the sensation felt at the vulva (dry, moist, wet, slippery) and the appearance of any mucus (sticky, creamy, clear and stretchy). Early dry or unchanged days give way to a build-up of fertile-type mucus. Peak Day is the last day of the slippery, fertile-type mucus, recognized once the dry days return.
