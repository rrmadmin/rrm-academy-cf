Peak Day and three high temperatures agree, window closes

Each cycle day records three signals. The hormone monitor climbs from low to peak as estrogen and the LH surge build, cervical mucus reaches its most fertile, slippery type on Peak Day, and the basal temperature steps up and holds after ovulation. When the mucus peak and three higher temperatures both confirm, the fertile window closes. One sign can cross-check another when either is unclear.
