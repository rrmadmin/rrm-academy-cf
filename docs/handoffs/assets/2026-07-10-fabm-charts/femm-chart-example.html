FEMM is a sympto-hormonal method: each day a woman logs her bleeding, her cervical mucus (dry, damp, wet, stretchy, estrogenic), and, once testing begins around cycle day 7, a urinary LH test. Estrogenic mucus opens the fertile window; a positive LH test marks the surge that triggers ovulation within about a day. Peak Day is the last day of clear, stretchy, estrogenic mucus.
