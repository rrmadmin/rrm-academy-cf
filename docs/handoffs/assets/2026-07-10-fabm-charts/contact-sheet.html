Proposed figcaption This illustrative example charts one cycle of electronic hormone monitor readings in Marquette Model notation, moving from Low through High to Peak across the fertile window. A trained Marquette instructor teaches you to interpret your own monitor readings.
Reviewer notes
  • accuracy: Chart begins the post-ovulatory infertile band on day 18 (immediately after the last Peak). Real Marquette rules close the fertile window at Peak+3/+4, not the day after Peak. However, the page's own howItWorks prose states the identical simplification ('After the Peak reading, the post-ovulatory infertile phase begins'), so the chart does NOT contradict the page; this is a shared simplification, not a blocker. | fix: Optional: if precision is ever wanted, shade days 18-20 as a transition/count-of-3 before the fully-infertile band, and update the page prose to match. Not required; chart and page are currently consistent with each other.
  • accuracy: Monitor reading sequence goes Peak (16-17) directly to Low (18+). A real Clearblue Advanced Fertility Monitor usually returns to High for ~2 days after the two Peak days before dropping to Low. The page never teaches a post-Peak High, so there is no page contradiction; this is a defensible illustrative simplification and the chart is labeled 'Illustrative example, not a real patient chart.' | fix: No change needed for page-consistency. If closer real-device fidelity is desired later, insert 2 High readings on days 18-19 before Lows resume.
  • accuracy: The 'Peak Day' callout points at day 16 (the first of two Peak readings). In mucus-based FABMs 'Peak Day' conventionally means the LAST day of peak signs; the monitor sense (first LH-surge detection) is defensible. The page does not define 'Peak Day', so no contradiction. | fix: Optional clarity: label it 'First Peak reading (LH surge detected)' to avoid conflation with the mucus-method 'Peak Day' definition. Not a blocker.
  • originality: Body text states 'after the Peak reading the post-ovulatory infertile phase begins,' and the fertile-window underline ends at day 17 (the last Peak day). The actual Marquette Model rule for avoiding pregnancy treats infertility as beginning on Peak+3 (evening of the 3rd day after Peak), not immediately after the Peak reading. This is a simplification, not one of the five IP/content-safety checks, and the piece is explicitly labeled an illustrative example, so it is not a blocker. | fix: Optionally soften to 'the days after Peak begin the return to the post-ovulatory infertile phase' or add a small note that confirmation takes a few days after Peak, to avoid implying instant post-Peak infertility.
