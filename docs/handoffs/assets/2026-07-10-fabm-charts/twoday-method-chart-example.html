Each day asks one thing: were any secretions noticed today, or yesterday? A yes to either marks the day potentially fertile. On days 11 and 17 nothing is noticed that day, yet each stays fertile because the day before had secretions, the look-back that gives the method its name. Bleeding hides secretions, so menstrual days are treated with the same caution. That caution carries one day past the bleeding: day 5 is dry but stays potentially fertile because day 4 was still a bleeding day, so day 6, the second dry day in a row, is the first infertile day.
