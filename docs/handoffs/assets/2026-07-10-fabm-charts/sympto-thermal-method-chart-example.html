Sympto-Thermal Method Chart: One Example Cycle

One cycle charted day by day: a basal body temperature line with its coverline and post-ovulation shift, cross-checked against the cervical mucus pattern the way this method reads two signs together.

Each morning she records her waking temperature and her cervical mucus. Mucus builds to Peak Day, the last day of the most fertile-type mucus. After ovulation, progesterone lifts the temperature so it holds above the coverline. The fertile window stays open until both signs agree: three temperatures above the coverline and the third day past Peak.
