Peak reading · LH surge

Each morning the monitor reads urinary estrogen (E3G) and LH from a test strip and shows one level: Low, High, or Peak. Low is the pre-fertile phase, High signals rising estrogen as ovulation approaches, and Peak marks the LH surge. The High and Peak days together define the fertile window; after the Peak reading the post-ovulatory infertile phase begins.
