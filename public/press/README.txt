USAGE
-----
Please do:
  - Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the letter "R".
  - Use the purple wordmark on light backgrounds and the white wordmark on dark.
  - Scale the logo proportionally.
Please don't:
  - Recolor, stretch, rotate, or add effects to the logo.
  - Recreate the wordmark in another typeface.
  - Place the purple wordmark on a busy or low-contrast background.
