# Author Outreach — Reading List

**Status:** DRAFT, held for Brian's approval. Nothing sent. Gmail tooling is draft-only.
**From:** Dr. Naomi Whittaker, from her own account (NOT community@ — Brian's call 2026-06-26; confirm exact address)
**Frame (corrected):** This is a heads-up *before* we publish, inviting the author in, not a notice after the fact. Lead with a genuine, specific personal connection to the author or their book. Each email is hand-written to that person, not a mail-merge.
**The ask:** "We're planning to feature your book; before it goes live, would you like to add the focus you'd want a reader to hold?" We publish it as a *From the author* note, credited and linked.
**Tone:** warm, peer-to-peer, specific. No corporate voice. No em dashes. The page keeps its honest framing notes; the email never raises them. If an author objects, handle 1:1.

---

## Skeleton (not a fill-in-the-blanks; the personalization IS the email)

1. **Open with the real connection** — the podcast, the fellowship, the patient who arrived holding their book. Something true and specific to them. Never start with "I'm Dr. Whittaker and we have a list."
2. **The before-publication frame** — "We're about to publish a curated reading list... and I wanted to come to you *before* it goes live, not after."
3. **Why their book is on it** — one specific, genuine sentence that proves we read it.
4. **The invitation** — a sentence or two on the focus they'd want a reader to hold; we feature it as a *From the author* note, credited and linked.
5. **Close** — promise the link when live; warm, optional invitation to share. Sign personally.
